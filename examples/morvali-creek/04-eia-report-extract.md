### Chapter 3. Baseline

3.2 Ambient air, surface water, soil and noise were monitored from 1 March to 31 May 2018. The data were generated for an earlier proposal at the same site and are adopted here.

3.7.1 Flora. Scrub and fallow land, with teak plantation in the Reserved Forest to the east.

3.7.2 Fauna. Common farmland birds and small mammals were recorded. Waterfowl use Morvali Creek in winter. Nilapani Bird Sanctuary, notified in 1987 under the Wild Life (Protection) Act 1972, lies about 3.2 km north-east of the plant boundary (Figure 3.4).

### Chapter 4. Impacts

4.3 The mill will operate on zero liquid discharge, so no impact on Morvali Creek is predicted.
