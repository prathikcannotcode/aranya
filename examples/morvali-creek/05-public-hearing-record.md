# Public hearing record

SYNTHETIC. Invented for testing Aranya. Not a real document.

## Notice

- Company's request to SPCB with the draft EIA report: 14 August 2023.
- Advertised by SPCB once, on 21 August 2023, in The Suvarnadesh Times (English). The notice fixes the hearing for 2 September 2023, 11:00, Morvali Gram Panchayat hall, and says the draft EIA report may be seen at the SPCB Regional Office.

## Minutes, 2 September 2023

- Presided over by the Additional District Magistrate, Rasulgarh, assisted by the SPCB Regional Officer.
- 41 people attended. The Company's representative presented the project.
- The secretary of the Sangh asked for the summary EIA in the local language. The Company's representative replied that only the English summary had been prepared.
- Fishers objected that any effluent would reach the creek. The Company replied that the mill would run on zero liquid discharge.
- The minutes were read over in the local language and signed by the Additional District Magistrate.
