# SPCB correspondence

SYNTHETIC. Invented for testing Aranya. Not a real document.

**28 August 2024. SPCB to Company.** Consent to Operate granted, valid to 31 March 2029. The Company reports commissioning on 3 September 2024.

**9 October 2024. Sangh to SPCB.** Since the last week of September, black water smelling of molasses has entered the creek through a pipe at Survey No. 214. Our members found dead fish at Kalmi ghat on 2 and 6 October and catches have fallen. We read about the clearance in the newspaper in February this year and were told no water would leave the mill. Please act.

**22 October 2024. SPCB inspection report RO/SD/24/212.** A bypass line runs from the equalisation tank to the creek at Survey No. 214. Discharge was running during the inspection. BOD at the outfall 1,480 mg/L. Direction issued under section 33A of the Water Act to remove the line within 15 days.

**6 January 2025. Sangh to SPCB.** The discharge continues. We have had no reply.

**3 February 2026. SPCB inspection report RO/SD/26/019.** The outfall at Survey No. 214 was running again. BOD 1,120 mg/L. Show-cause notice issued.

**14 July 2026. Sangh to SPCB.** About three tonnes of fish dead at Kalmi ghat this morning.

**16 July 2026. SPCB inspection report RO/SD/26/144.** Fish kill confirmed. The bypass line was in use. Samples sent for analysis.
