# Environmental Clearance

SYNTHETIC. Invented for testing Aranya. Not a real document.

State Environment Impact Assessment Authority, Suvarnadesh
No. SEIAA/SD/2024/0117, dated 12 February 2024
To: Tarangi Sugars Pvt. Ltd., Survey Nos. 211 to 216, Morvali village, Rasulgarh Taluka

Subject: Environmental Clearance for a 6,500 TCD greenfield sugar mill

1. The proposal falls under item 5(j) of the Schedule to the EIA Notification 2006 (sugar industry) and was processed as Category B. SEAC screened it as B1 on 6 April 2022, and Terms of Reference issued on 18 April 2022.
2. The proponent states in Form 1 that no protected area lies within 15 km of the site.
3. The public hearing was held on 2 September 2023. The final EIA report was submitted on 20 October 2023. SEAC appraised the proposal at its 71st meeting on 14 December 2023 and recommended clearance.
4. SEIAA grants Environmental Clearance under the EIA Notification 2006, subject to the conditions below.

## Specific conditions

- S1. Zero liquid discharge. No process effluent, condensate or wash water shall leave the premises at any time.
- S2. Continuous online effluent monitoring connected to the SPCB server.
- S3. Effluent storage lagoons shall be lined, with capacity for 30 days.

## General conditions

- G1. Greenbelt on at least 33% of the plot within three years of commissioning.
- G2. Six-monthly compliance reports to SEIAA and the Regional Office.
- G3. The proponent shall publish this clearance in two newspapers within seven days. SEIAA shall upload it to its website.
- G4. Any appeal against this clearance lies to the National Green Tribunal under section 16 of the National Green Tribunal Act 2010, within 30 days.

## Endorsements

- Uploaded to the SEIAA website: 16 February 2024.
- Company's newspaper notices: 19 February 2024, The Suvarnadesh Times and Suvarna Patrika.
