# Creek surveys

SYNTHETIC. Invented for testing Aranya. Not a real document.

Both surveys by the Rasulgarh College of Fisheries. Service index: composite of benthic diversity, fish density and water quality, scored against the Kalmi reference reach (reference reach = 1.00).

## Pre-project survey, November 2023

- Lower Morvali Creek scored 0.58.
- The College attributes the shortfall to the untreated Rasulgarh municipal sewage outfall, about 4 km upstream of the survey reach, and to sand mining.

## Post-incident survey, August 2026

- Affected reach mapped by GPS polygon: 42.0 ha.
- Service index across the polygon: 0.15.
- Fish density down by about four-fifths against November 2023.
