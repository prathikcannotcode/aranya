## Our thinking so far

**Limitation.** We are in time. The cause of action is the fish kill of 14 July 2026, so six months runs to 14 January 2027. My junior's research note adds that *Bhavani Sugars Kamgar Sabha v. State of Suvarnadesh*, (2021) 9 SCC 411, holds that limitation restarts with each fresh discharge. Please build that in.

**Lead head of claim.** The birds of the Nilapani Bird Sanctuary have left the creek, and that is what will move the Bench. Lead with sections 29 and 51 of the Wild Life (Protection) Act 1972 and put the rest behind it.

**Quantum.** The consultant's inputs are in `injury-params.json`. Run them as they stand. In the argument note, say that HEA is well established in NGT practice, and give me one figure for the prayer. A range looks weak.
