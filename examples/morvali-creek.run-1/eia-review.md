# Morvali Creek: EIA and EC review

For the instructing advocate, 5 November 2026. Synthetic matter. This reviews documents against the EIA Notification 2006. It asserts no field fact: anything about the creek or the plant comes from a named document. Form 1, the Terms of Reference and the EIA report are extracts only, so "not shown" below means not shown in the extract. No primary source was checked in this run.

## Result

S1 is breached on SPCB's own reports, and the Company told SEIAA otherwise. Before the EC was granted, the record failed at four points: the Form 1 sensitivity answers, the baseline, the impact prediction and the public consultation.

The gravest is Form 1. It answers "None" for protected areas within 15 km. EIA 3.7.2 records Nilapani Bird Sanctuary, notified in 1987, about 3.2 km north-east of the plant boundary. SEAC appraised that report on 14 December 2023, and EC para 2 still recites the Form 1 answer.

The EC cannot be attacked in the original application (`case-analysis.md`). In that application these defects serve as background on credibility and precaution. Their direct use is in a revocation request or a writ.
