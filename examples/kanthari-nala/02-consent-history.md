To: Sarvani Textile Processors Pvt. Ltd.

"With reference to your letter of 2 March 2016, the Authority has examined the proposal for a cotton and synthetic fabric dyeing, printing and finishing unit of 60,000 metres a day at Plot 14, Ishanpur Industrial Area, Kantharwadi. You must obtain consents from the State Pollution Control Board."

## Hazardous waste authorisation

ETP sludge (category 35.3 in the Unit's authorisation) goes to the Common Hazardous Waste Treatment, Storage and Disposal Facility at Ishanpur. The authorisation was renewed with each consent. The KSPCB's 2025 joint inspection report checked every manifest from April 2022 to July 2025 against the facility's receipts: 61 manifests, all matched, no quantity gaps.

## Boiler stack monitoring

Quarterly stack monitoring by a recognised laboratory, 2022 to 2025: particulate matter 62 to 88 mg/Nm3 against a consent limit of 150; SO2 and NOx within limits in every quarter. The KSPCB has raised no air-quality point at any inspection.
