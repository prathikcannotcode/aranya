# The Unit's appeal

SYNTHETIC. Every party, place, date and document in this matter is invented for testing Aranya.

## Cause title

Appeal No. 118 of 2025 (CZ), Sarvani Textile Processors Pvt. Ltd. v. Kesarbhumi State Pollution Control Board, before the National Green Tribunal, Central Zone Bench. Filed on 14 November 2025 against the direction of 2 August 2025 and the order of 10 October 2025.

## I.A. No. 1 of 2025: condonation of delay

"The impugned direction was communicated to the Appellant on 11 August 2025. The period of thirty days expired on 10 September 2025. The appeal is filed on 14 November 2025, with a delay of 65 days, caused by the festival holidays and the illness of the Appellant's environmental consultant. The delay may kindly be condoned in the interest of justice."

The Tribunal issued notice on the delay application on 3 December 2025. It has not decided it. The appeal is listed on 2 December 2026 for hearing on the delay application and, if condoned, on interim relief.

## Grounds (extract)

A. The closure is disproportionate: the borewell has been sealed and the Unit employs 640 workers.

B. The order of 10 October 2025 is without jurisdiction. In *Delhi Pollution Control Committee v. Lodhi Property Co. Ltd.*, 2025 INSC 923, the Supreme Court held that Boards may levy restitutionary and compensatory damages under section 33A only after rules or regulations set out the method and procedure (para 39(c)). No such rules have been framed in Kesarbhumi.

C. The 2023 bypass was a one-off maintenance lapse, already compensated.

## KSPCB counter-affidavit, 15 June 2026 (extract)

"Ground B is answered by the order of the Hon'ble Supreme Court dated 21 May 2026 in C.A. 757-760 of 2013, which clarifies that the directions of 4 August 2025 'should not be understood to mean that the regulators, being the Pollution Control Boards cannot proceed to impose and collect the compensatory environmental damages in the absence of subordinate legislation'. A copy of the order is not annexed, as it is available in the public domain."
