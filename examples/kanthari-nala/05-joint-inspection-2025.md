# Joint inspection, groundwater results and second closure, 2025

SYNTHETIC. Every party, place, date and document in this matter is invented for testing Aranya.

## The Samiti's complaint, 5 March 2025

To the KSPCB and the Collector, Ishanpur: eleven drinking-water borewells in Kantharwadi and Ratnapada have turned reddish-brown since January 2025, and the health centre is seeing skin complaints. The Samiti asks for an inspection and safe drinking water.

## Joint inspection report, 18 July 2025

Team: Regional Officer, KSPCB; Senior Geologist, Groundwater Survey and Development Agency, Kesarbhumi (GSDA); Tahsildar, Sonvel.

1. Behind the ETP, under a loose steel plate, a 150 mm borewell about 55 m (180 feet) deep. A submersible pump in the ETP's final collection sump fed a pipe into the borewell casing. Dye residue coated the casing and the plate.
2. The Unit produced a contractor's invoice of 1 February 2025 for "drilling and casing of recharge bore, 180 ft". It said the bore was for "rainwater recharge". The ETP logbook records "sump transfer to RB" on 154 days between 3 February and 17 July 2025.
3. GSDA sampled 46 borewells within 2 km of the Unit across the three villages. 31 exceed the acceptable limits of IS 10500 for colour or total dissolved solids. 9 exceed the acceptable limit for total chromium (0.05 mg/L), with a highest reading of 0.31 mg/L in a Ratnapada well 600 m down-gradient of the Unit.
4. GSDA's note: "The direction and extent of the contaminant plume are not known. A hydrogeological investigation by an expert institution, with monitoring wells, is needed before any remediation can be designed or costed."

All samples were sealed and signed by the team and the Unit's representative.

## Direction of 2 August 2025

Issued by the Member Secretary, KSPCB, under section 33A of the Water Act 1974: closure of the Unit, disconnection of electricity and water, and sealing of the borewell. The direction also requires the Unit to fund drinking water for the three villages until the KSPCB decides otherwise.

The direction was emailed to the Unit's registered address on 4 August 2025 at 18:42. The Unit's appeal says the signed copy reached its office by post on 11 August 2025. The Unit has been closed since 5 August 2025.
