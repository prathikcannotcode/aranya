# Village surveys

SYNTHETIC. Every party, place, date and document in this matter is invented for testing Aranya.

## Borewells

From the GSDA results of July 2025 (`05-joint-inspection-2025.md`), re-sampled by GSDA in March 2026:

| Village | Area (ha) | Borewells sampled | Above IS 10500 for colour or TDS | Above for total chromium |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Kantharwadi | 260 | 19 | 14 | 4 |
| Ratnapada | 210 | 17 | 13 | 5 |
| Bhilkhed | 170 | 10 | 4 | 0 |
| Total | 640 | 46 | 31 | 9 |

The March 2026 re-sampling shows the same wells failing, and two more Bhilkhed wells above the colour limit. GSDA repeats that the plume has not been mapped, that no monitoring wells exist, and that it has no data on aquifer depth, flow or extent in the area. No hydrogeological study has been commissioned.

## Drinking water

Kantharwadi Gram Panchayat has supplied tanker water to the three villages since 1 April 2025, at its own cost: Rs 18.6 lakh to 31 October 2026. The Unit paid nothing towards it under the direction of 2 August 2025.

## Health

Primary Health Centre, Kantharwadi: dermatitis cases from the three villages were 38 in 2023, 74 in 2024 and 212 in 2025. The Medical Officer's letter of 12 January 2026 says the rise "coincides with the change in well water" and recommends testing; no study has been done. Members spent Rs 3.1 lakh on treatment in 2025, by the Samiti's own count from receipts.

## Crops

85 acres of tomato and chilli were irrigated from affected wells in the 2025 rabi season. The Taluka Agriculture Officer's panchnama of 14 February 2026 records yield losses of 40 to 60% and leaf burn consistent with saline irrigation water.

## Fishing
