# Restoration proposal: Kanthari Nala

SYNTHETIC. Every party, place, date and document in this matter is invented for testing Aranya.

Prepared for the Samiti by an environmental consultant, 28 October 2026. Draft for counsel.

## The injured stretch

From the Unit's storm-water outfall to the Sonvel confluence: 2.4 km of channel, with an average wetted width and bank zone of about 30 m. Total injured area: 18 acres.

The reference reach is 3 km of Kanthari Nala upstream of the industrial area, surveyed in October 2026: similar width, gradient and substrate, no industrial outfall.

## Service levels

Benthic macroinvertebrate diversity (Shannon index), sediment chromium and riparian cover were scored against the reference reach:

- Reference reach (baseline): 0.9 of an undisturbed nala of this type, because of farm run-off upstream.
- Injured stretch: 0.35, from the October 2026 survey. Sediment chromium is 410 mg/kg against 38 mg/kg in the reference reach.

## Timing

- The injury is taken to start in June 2023, when the bypass began (`04-ocems-and-production.md`), and to have continued for three years to the 2026 survey.
- With restoration, the stretch is expected to recover to baseline over eight years.
- Restoration (sediment removal in hot spots, bank stabilisation, riparian planting) on a comparable stretch nearby would yield a gain of 0.5 a hectare over twenty years, starting three years after the injury began, once works are complete.

## Cost

Restoration cost on recent comparable works in the district: Rs 6.5 lakh per hectare.

## What this proposal does not cover

Groundwater. The aquifer cannot be scored like a stretch of channel: nothing is known about the plume's extent, depth or flow (`08-village-surveys.md`). Remediation needs a hydrogeological investigation by an expert institution first, and I have not attempted it.
