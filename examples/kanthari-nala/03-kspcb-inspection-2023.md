# KSPCB inspection, closure and first compensation order, 2023 to 2024

SYNTHETIC. Every party, place, date and document in this matter is invented for testing Aranya.

## Inspection report, Regional Officer, Ishanpur, 14 September 2023

Inspected 11:20 to 15:40 with the Unit's ETP in-charge present throughout.

1. The aeration tank blowers were switched off. The ETP in-charge said they had been "off for maintenance since morning".
2. A 150 mm HDPE pipe ran from the equalisation tank, past the treatment units, to the storm-water drain at the north boundary. The drain outfalls into Kanthari Nala 40 m downstream. Dark red effluent was flowing in it at the time.
3. The OCEMS flow meter at the final outlet showed 0.0 m3/hour.
4. Samples were taken at the bypass outlet and at the nala 100 m downstream, split in three, sealed with the KSPCB seal and the Unit's seal, and signed by the Regional Officer and the ETP in-charge. One set was handed to the Unit. Chain-of-custody forms are attached to the report.

Results (KSPCB laboratory, report of 22 September 2023, bypass outlet):

| Parameter | Result | Schedule A limit |
|---|---|---|
| pH | 10.9 | 6.5 to 8.5 |
| BOD | 410 mg/L | 30 |
| COD | 1,840 mg/L | 250 |
| Total chromium | 2.6 mg/L | 2.0 |
| Colour | Dark red at 1:50 dilution | Not perceptible at 1:10 |

The Unit's own set, tested at a laboratory of its choice, gave COD 1,710 mg/L and total chromium 2.4 mg/L. The Unit has never disputed the sampling.

## Direction of 3 October 2023

Issued by the Member Secretary, KSPCB, under section 33A of the Water Act 1974: closure of the Unit and disconnection of its electricity and water supply. Served and acknowledged the same day.

## Order of 12 January 2024

After the Unit removed the bypass pipe, repaired the blowers and furnished a bank guarantee of Rs 25 lakh, the KSPCB:

1. revoked the direction of 3 October 2023;
2. granted the Consent to Operate described in `02-consent-history.md`; and
3. levied environmental compensation for the bypass "in accordance with the CPCB methodology":

| Factor | Value used | KSPCB's basis |

The Unit paid on 30 January 2024. The order says nothing about the period before 14 September 2023 or about the lapse of consent from 1 April 2022.
