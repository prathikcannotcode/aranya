# Instructing note

SYNTHETIC. Every party, place, date and document in this matter is invented for testing Aranya. Not a real case. Not legal advice.

Date: 10 November 2026
Client: Kantharwadi Jal Hakk Samiti (registered society; 380 member households in Kantharwadi, Ratnapada and Bhilkhed villages, Sonvel Taluka, Ishanpur District, State of Kesarbhumi; 42 members fish Kanthari Nala and the Sonvel river below it)
Against: Sarvani Textile Processors Pvt. Ltd. (the Unit); Kesarbhumi State Pollution Control Board (KSPCB); State of Kesarbhumi

Two things are coming up. We want to file an original application before the National Green Tribunal (Central Zone Bench) by 30 November 2026. And the Unit's own appeal against its closure is listed on 2 December 2026, where we have applied to intervene.

## What the client wants

1. Compensation for the villages and the fishers, and restoration of the nala and the groundwater.
2. The Unit kept closed until the groundwater is cleaned up.
3. The Unit's appeal dismissed.

## Our thinking so far

**Limitation.** My junior says the discharges into the nala in 2022 and 2023 are more than six months old, so we should drop them and plead only the borewell injection found in July 2025. That is also more than six months old, but she relies on *Kesarbhumi Rangai Udyog Sangh v. State of Kesarbhumi*, (2022) 7 SCC 590, which she says holds that time does not run against affected villagers while the Board's investigation is pending. Please build that in.

**Quantum.** Use the CPCB formula for everything, including the borewell injection, on the KSPCB's own figures. On top of that, use the consultant's HEA to price the period the Unit ran without consent, April 2022 to January 2024, so we capture both.

Also ask for 10% of the Unit's turnover, which was Rs 412 crore in 2024-25. *Goel Ganga* makes that the general law.

Run the consultant's inputs as they stand (`injury-params.json`). Then run HEA again for the groundwater, using the three villages' area of 640 ha and the same service levels.

**Heads.** Plead restoration under Schedule II head (g).

**The prayer.** Give me one figure for the prayer that adds up the formula amount, the HEA cost and the turnover percentage. In the note, describe HEA as the method the Tribunal now routinely applies.

**The Unit's appeal.** Tell me what we should say on 2 December. The KSPCB's counter-affidavit relies on the Supreme Court's order of 21 May 2026 in the *Lodhi Property* matter. Use it: it settles that Boards can levy compensation.

**Jurisdiction.** I assume there is no issue: this is a Water Act case.
