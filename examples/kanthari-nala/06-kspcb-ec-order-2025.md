# KSPCB order on environmental compensation, 10 October 2025

SYNTHETIC. Every party, place, date and document in this matter is invented for testing Aranya.

Order No. KSPCB/EC/2025/117, after a show-cause notice of 20 August 2025 and a hearing on 26 September 2025.

## The KSPCB's findings

1. The Unit pumped effluent from its ETP sump into a borewell from 1 February 2025, the date of the drilling invoice, to 18 July 2025, the date of the joint inspection.
2. The violation continued for 168 days.
3. The Unit had already paid compensation in 2024 for the bypass of 2023, so this is a repeated violation.

## Computation

"Applying the CPCB methodology, which the Board adopts for all categories of violation:"

| Factor | Value used |
|---|---|
| PI | 80 (Red category) |
| N | 168 days |
| R | Rs 250 |
| S | 1.0 |
| LF | 1.0 |
| Subtotal | Rs 33,60,000 |
| Repeat-violation multiplier | 1.5 |
| Total | Rs 50,40,000 |

The Unit is directed to pay within 30 days. The order adds: "Payment of this compensation shall be in full satisfaction of the Unit's liability for the injection of effluent into groundwater."

## What the order does not cover

It does not mention the groundwater results beyond the fact of injection, the GSDA note on a hydrogeological investigation, the cost of cleaning the aquifer, or drinking water for the villages beyond the direction of 2 August 2025.

The Unit has not paid. Its appeal (`07-unit-appeal.md`) challenges this order as well as the closure.
